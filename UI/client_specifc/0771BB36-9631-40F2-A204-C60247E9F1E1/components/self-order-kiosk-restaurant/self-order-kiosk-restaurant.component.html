Intuitive Self Ordering Kiosk
for Restaurants

Placing a kiosk machine in your restaurant improves service quality and speed, boosting sales. It automates the ordering process, and you can easily tailor the kiosk menu interface through our portal.

  • Place Order - Customers easily browse menu items and place order with customizable options
  • Smart Upsell - Suggests add ons or combos, encourage customers to order more.
  • Make Payment - Customers pay with their preferred methods, either cash or contactless payment.
  • Orders Sent Directly to POS and Kitchen - Improve preparation accuracy and speed.

Innovative Self-Service
Checkout Kiosk for Retail

Retailers are adopting kiosks to reduce queues, personalize the shopping experience, and enhance customer service. APPZPOS Self Order Kiosk features a functional design and a premium touchscreen, providing an excellent user experience with integrated payment solutions.

  • Browse Products - customers easily browse products via the kiosk and add chosen items to the cart with a quick scan.
  • Make Payment - Customers pay with their preferred methods, either cash or contactless payment.
  • Collect Item - Get a printed receipt after payment and collect your item in-store.
