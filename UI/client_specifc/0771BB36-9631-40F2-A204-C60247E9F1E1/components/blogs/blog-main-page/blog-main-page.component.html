What is BCRS in Singapore? A Complete Guide for F&B Businesses (2026 Update)

  • 18th Mar 2026

The Beverage Container Return Scheme (BCRS) is a nationwide recycling initiative introduced by the National Environment Agency (NEA) to reduce waste and improve recycling rates. Starting 1 April 2026, all regulated pre-packaged beverages in Singapore will include a 10-cent refundable deposit.
