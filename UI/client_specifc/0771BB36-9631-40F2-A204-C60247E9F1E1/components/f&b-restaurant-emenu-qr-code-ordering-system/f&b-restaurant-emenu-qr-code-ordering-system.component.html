Smart Menu: Fast, Efficient Ordering
with QR Code Order

  • Easily Customize Your QR Code Menus Remotely from Portal and do updates instantly
  • Customers can order from multiple devices and have it linked to the same bill or separate bill
  • Each QR code is link to a specific table number
  • Two QR options: Static or Dynamic Qr Code Ordering
  • Static QR codes are reusable and can be placed on a specific table for easy access.
  • Dynamic QR codes offer extra security, requiring a unique passcode generated by the POS system for customer orders.

How does QR Menu Ordering work?

  • Scan QR code label - Customers scan the QR code with their phones to browse the menu.
  • Place order - Customers select their dishes and add to the cart with their specific table number.
  • Make payment - Customers pay with their preferred payment methods via their phones.
  • Order sent to POS and kitchen upon payment completion.
