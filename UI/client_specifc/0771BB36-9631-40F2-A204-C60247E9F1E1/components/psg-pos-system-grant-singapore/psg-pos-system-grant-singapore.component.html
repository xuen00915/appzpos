EDGE & PSG: your questions answered

What is the EDGE Grant?

EDGE is Enterprise Singapore’s grant for business upgrading, digitalisation and overseas growth. Launched on 30 September 2026, it brings together the Enterprise Development Grant (EDG), Productivity Solutions Grant (PSG) and Market Readiness Assistance (MRA).

For a POS project, check the relevant Automation & Digitalisation activity. Eligibility and support depend on that activity. Explore the official EDGE Grant guide.

What happened to PSG? Can I still apply?

PSG means Productivity Solutions Grant. It closed to new applications on 29 September 2026. From 30 September 2026, apply under EDGE for a new qualifying project.

That is why this page now refers to EDGE, even if you found us by searching for “PSG POS”. Read Enterprise Singapore’s PSG update.

I already applied for PSG. What happens now?

Applications submitted before 30 September 2026 continue to be assessed under PSG’s requirements. Ongoing PSG projects remain supported through completion and claim disbursement. Follow your existing grant conditions and claim deadlines. Read the official transition FAQs.

How much grant support can I receive?
  • Eligible SMEs may receive up to 50% support for qualifying digitalisation costs. The approved activity, eligible costs and applicable caps determine the final amount. Funding is reimbursed after project completion and full payment; it is not an upfront discount.
