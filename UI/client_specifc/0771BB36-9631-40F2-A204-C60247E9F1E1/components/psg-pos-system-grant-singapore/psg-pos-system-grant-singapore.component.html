Up to 50% Monetary Support To Kickstart Your F&B Business

SMEs are eligible for up to 50% Productivity Solutions Grant (PSG) support for the adoption of APPZPOS F&B Point of Sales, a Pre-Approved Solution under the IMDA SMEs Go Digital programme.

PSG Pre-approved Vendor

  • APPZPOS is proud to be a Pre-Approved F&B Solution with PSG support
    (grant up to 50% support).
  • Eligible business can sign up for Solutions packages & save up to 50%
    (of qualified amount) on our following products
  • Applicable POS Module: Digital Ordering System (POS System), Table QR Ordering, Order Hub (Delivery Integration), Self Ordering Kiosk

PSG Eligibility Requirements

  • SME(s) Registered and operating in Singapore
  • Purchase/lease/subscription of the IT solutions/equipment/
    consultancy service must be used in Singapore
  • (For selected solutions only) Have a minimum of 30% local shareholding

How to Apply PSG Grant for our POS System

  • 1
    Get A Quote from Us
  • 2
    Apply PSG Grant under BGP
  • 3
    Claim for Reimbursement

Book a slot for demo and we will be in touch shortly to discuss available solution packages that best suit your business.
A quote will be given based on your requirements.

Check whether you are eligible for a PSG grant and we will assist you to submit an application on Business Grant Portal (BGP).
Registration for a CorpPass account is also needed for transactions.

After receiving approval, we will arrange installation for your preferable date.
Afterward, submit a claim to Enterprise Singapore for reimbursement of your approved costs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What will be the reimbursement amount ?

  • Eligible businesses can sign up for Solutions packages & save up to 50% of the qualifying amount.

Q: What are the packages you provide ?

  • Currently, we have 5 available PSG packages.
  • Package 1 - 1 Digital Ordering + 1 QR Order
  • Package 2 - 1 Digital Ordering + 1 QR Order + 1 Order Hub
  • Package 3 - 1 Digital Ordering + 1 QR Order + 1 Kiosk
  • Package 4 - 1 Digital Ordering + 1 QR Order + 2 Kiosk
  • Package 5 - 1 Digital Ordering + 1 QR Order + 1 Order Hub + 1 Kiosk

Q: Who can apply ?

  • Business entities can apply for PSG if they meet the following criteria,
  • SME(s) Registered and operating in Singapore
  • Purchase/lease/subscription of the IT solutions/equipment/consultancy service must be used in Singapore
  • (For selected solutions only) Have a minimum of 30% local shareholding

Q: Where can I apply for Productivity Solutions Grant ?

  • After you have obtained a quotation from your preferred vendor for the supportable solution(s), please log into Business Grant Portal to submit your application for PSG.

Q: Why Appzgate Solutions ?

  • Incorporated in 2016, Appzgate Solutions has been providing Restaurant/Fast Food Cloud POS Solutions to many F&B and retail customers.
  • Singapore based support team.
  • Advanced features to manage smooth operation and reporting.
  • PSG Grant Pre-Approved solutions which helps SME to save on the software and implementation cost.

Q: How to Apply ?

  • Decide the best supportable solution for your business needs and obtain a quotation from us.
  • Submit an application on the Business Grant Portal, but ensure you register for a CorpPass account before transacting on the portal.
  • Keep track of the application status until you receive an offer letter.
  • Proceed with payment to us, and we will start arranging the installation for you.
  • Contact our team today to get more information
