Manage All Your Delivery Orders into one APPZPOS System

  • Customers can access your restaurant's online ordering system and place orders through various food ordering platforms.
  • All orders from food ordering platforms are automatically collected and managed in one Cloud POS to avoid multiple handling.
  • Display driver & customer contact details from each platform in the POS system for custom order handling and easy communication.
  • Easily customize menus, update availability, and run promotions across all delivery platforms using the APPZPOS portal.
  • Integrate directly with POS and KDS systems. Receive your delivery orders directly into the POS system and send them straight to the kitchen.
  • Easily monitor all your delivery, online and in-store activities, and get a real-time overview of restaurant operations.
  • Currently integrated with GrabFood, Foodpanda, and Deliveroo, and expanding to more platforms soon.
