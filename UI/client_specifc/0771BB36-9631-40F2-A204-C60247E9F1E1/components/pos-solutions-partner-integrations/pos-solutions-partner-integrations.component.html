GTO Mall Integration

APPZPOS POS system installation offers free integration with the shopping mall GTO, enabling the automatic transmission of sales reports from the cloud POS to partner malls' accounting systems. With a one-time setup, businesses can effortlessly generate and send GTO sales reports at preferred schedules. This centralized management solution simplifies restaurant operations, providing a user-friendly interface for efficient sales data handling. APPZPOS also prioritizes security measures to safeguard sensitive sales data during transmission and storage.

With an APPZPOS package subscription, we also provide integration with top accounting software like QuickBooks and Xero, creating a seamless connection between the POS system and accounting software.
