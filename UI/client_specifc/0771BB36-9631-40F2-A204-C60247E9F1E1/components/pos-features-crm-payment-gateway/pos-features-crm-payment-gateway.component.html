Cloud Based POS System

APPZPOS is a cloud-based solution and as it is web modules it can be easily adoptable for different hardware screen sizes. We offer different hardware options and different sizes of screens to suit customers' requirements. With the cloud-based system, you can access your sales data, inventory, and reports anytime, anywhere.

Trusted Local Based Support

Our dedicated support team is here to help, ensuring your business experiences minimal downtime and runs smoothly. Our developers are all based in Singapore, ensuring you receive the best and prompt support tailored to your needs. We also provide free installation services and thorough professional training, making sure your team is well-prepared to make the most of our POS system and enjoy its full benefits for your business.

Customer Relationship Management Membership CRM

APPZPOS has strong CRM features to manage and grow the customer base by attracting customers with different marketing strategies. It includes a loyalty point system, tier-based customer logic and voucher option based on customer tier or specific customer. These features can be used in multiple portals and updated balance will be reflected to all portals without need of any separate integration.

Smart Inventory Management

APPZPOS has an inventory system to manage the quantity-based items. You can keep track of stock levels in real-time, set up automatic reordering, and gain insights into your best-selling products. For example, you can enable/disable an item to stop the overselling, or you can set an alert for the item, the system will alert you when the item is in lower stock.

Sales Analytics & Reporting

APPZPOS has a dashboard with a quick view on sales and other analytics. It also has many reports which helps the outlet to recognise their highest selling items, frequent visiting customers etc. From daily summaries to detailed sales trends which assist you in making business decisions.

Delivery Management System

Our POS system provides customers with a diverse selection of delivery options, ensuring efficient delivery to their chosen destinations. We have integrated with GrabExpress, Lalamove, and PandGo for delivery, eliminating the need for you to have your own drivers. Customers can effortlessly compare estimated delivery times and fares across these platforms or directly select their preferred delivery service for the most optimal experience.

Support Multiple Outlets

For those who operate multiple outlets, APPZPOS is your ultimate solution. APPZPOS provides you with the tools to maintain consistency and clarity across the entire spectrum of your operations. With that, you can experience seamless management across all your stores through a unified dashboard, guaranteeing uniformity and transparency throughout your operations.

Data Security

APPZPOS employs data security measures as essential safeguards that protect both the company and its customers from potential threats and breaches. We have implemented effective data security measures, including encryption, access controls, authentication protocols, monitoring systems,etc.
