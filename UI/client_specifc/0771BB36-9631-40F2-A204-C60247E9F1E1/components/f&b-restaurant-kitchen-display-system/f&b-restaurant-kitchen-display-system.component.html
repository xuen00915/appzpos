Speed Up Your Kitchen Operations with Real Time Order Updates

APPZPOS KDS System seamlessly integrated with the POS system, offering real-time updates and efficient communication to ensure rapid order processing and reduces customer wait times.

  • New Order Created Upon Payment.
  • Order Sent - Order sent to kitchen display / printer instantly, chefs begin preparation.
  • Order Served - Order served when the chefs move the order to Collect status

Multiple Stations with Individual Kitchen Printers

With APPZPOS, you can easily separate food, drinks, or dessert orders using individual printer stations to streamline kitchen operations. The system will automatically send your orders to the right station, allowing your chef to prepare the dishes efficiently.
